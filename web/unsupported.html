Parble needs WebGPU

This browser did not give the page a WebGPU adapter, and Parble has no WebGL fallback.

These run it:

  • Chrome or Edge 113 and later, on macOS, Windows or ChromeOS
  • Safari 26 and later, on macOS, iOS and iPadOS
  • Firefox 141 and later on Windows, or later versions on macOS

If your browser is on that list, check that hardware acceleration is turned on, then try again.
