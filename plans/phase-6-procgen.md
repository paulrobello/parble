# Phase 6: Procedural generation

PRD: `PRD.md` §6 P6. Phase goal: generated tracks that are always runnable, editable after generation, plus fill-between and infinite descent.

Design constraint: validation is a headless sim run at 240 Hz, tens of thousands of steps per track, and wasm is single-threaded. All generation and validation runs in a Web Worker built from `parble-sim-wasm` (no Bevy) in time-sliced batches, so the render thread never stalls.

## Tasks

### P6-T1 — Generator core
- `parble-procgen` grows a port graph on the grid from a hopper.
- Weighted piece choice from sliders: footprint, height, special-piece density, difficulty, allowed families.
- Collision-free footprint placement with backtracking. Supports and pillars placed under elevated pieces. Ends at a finish.
- Fully determined by (seed, params, catalog version).
- Verify: unit tests show the same seed + params give a byte-identical track, and generated tracks pass the P3-T2 validation engine with zero issues.
- Depends on: P2-T7, P3-T2.
- Status (2026-11-13): chain generator landed in `crates/parble-procgen` (`generate(defs, seed, params)`, `GENERATOR_VERSION` 1). The search places single-exit pieces and uses the validator's controlled-drop transfer (`L1` exit onto an `L3` inlet one layer below), so the `L1` hopper needs no lift. Pillar columns go under every elevated cell after the walk, which needed the validator to treat a support fed only by idle supports as idle. Specials generated: gate, checkpoint, rope arch. Not generated yet: splitter, toggle and join (branches need a second feed or a per-end finish), lifts, funnels, one-way holes, socket/spigot pieces.
- Measured physical pass rate (`cargo test -p parble-sim --release --test procgen_pass_rate -- --ignored --nocapture`, default params, every marble finishing within 60 s): 11 of 100 seeds (0 to 99) at the T1 commit. Marbles stall on rail curves and slopes, overshoot transfers entered above about 0.5 m/s and leave the track on high-speed rails. Static validity is the T1 bar; the pass rate is P6-T1b.
- P6-T1b and its follow-up (generator version 4): each open end carries an integer squared-speed estimate (`speed.rs`: gain per level, per-shape retention, catch and redirect reset values) and the search refuses steps the sim measured to fail (a transfer above 0.33 m/s, a turn above 0.5 m/s, a drop below 0.45 m/s, a creep below 0.17 m/s). `reach.rs` prunes any exit whose (level, speed, flat run) state has no way to a finish. Attempts are 2,000 nodes, so a bad early choice restarts sooner. `blk_drop_l3`, `rail_s_1x3_l1` and `rail_curve90_r90_l1` are left out. That reached 67 of 100 seeds (0 to 99), 72% over 300. The stall that capped it was a sim bug, not the kit: `ChannelSlope::holds` extends a slope's channel flat past its ports, so a gentle slope on a drop's row tagged the drop cell's floor triangles, and `covers` checks the plan only, so the catch's landing floor under the drop "covered" the marble above it and dropped those contacts (a marble at 0.5 m/s sank 6 mm and stopped 20 mm short of the bore). `ChannelSlope::reaches_height` now also requires the ball's height to be within the channel's range; regression test `a_slope_on_the_drops_row_does_not_stall_the_marble`. Final: 95 of 100 seeds (0 to 99), 281 of 300 (93.7%), no generation failures, every marble finishing within 60 s. The follow-up (marbles dropping through `L3` straights at 0.15 to 0.4 m/s, about 6% of tracks) was the same plan-only coverage in another branch: when a ball is over a slope's cell the bend branch clears the contacts of bend-tagged triangles, and a gentle slope four layers up in the same plan cell did that to the straights after a curve. That branch now uses `covers_from_above` (the ball is not below the slope's channel), the contact-clearing branch uses `holds_ball` (the ball is within its height range); the contact branches keep plan-only `covers` because a ball falls onto a landing floor from above. Regression test `a_slope_far_above_does_not_open_the_floor_of_straights_after_a_curve`. The generator also no longer starts a transfer straight from a catch's exit (4 of 18 failures). Now 294 of 300 (98%), 99 of 100 for seeds 0 to 99, no generation failures. Pass rate barely moves with length and the marble count does not matter.

### P6-T2 — Headless validation worker
- `parble-sim-wasm` exposes `generate_and_validate(seed, params, budget_ms)` with time slicing and resumable state.
- A Web Worker wrapper in TS.
- A failing candidate retries with the next sub-seed. A track is accepted only when every hopper marble reaches the finish inside a time limit.
- The final accepted seed is stored in the track metadata.
- Verify: Vitest worker tests. A Playwright run shows no main-thread frame over 25 ms during generation.
- Depends on: P6-T1, P0-T4.
- Status (2026-11-15): the job is `parble_sim::procgen_job` (validation needs the sim, so it lives in parble-sim, approved by Paul), sliced by `parble-sim-wasm`'s `generate_and_validate` in the `web/ui/src/gen` worker. The accepted seed is the track's own `seed`. The generator wasm has its own 2 MB gzip budget (1.33 MB), outside the 15 MB startup budget (approved by Paul).
- Long tracks (generator version 7): a walk descends about one port level per one and a half pieces, so at a flat-run limit of 3 a length above about seven pieces a layer of height ran out of height (90 pieces from layer 10 failed every seed). Above seven a layer the limit is now 8, and a length above 12 a layer (`GenParams::max_length`) is refused at once as `TooLong`, so the job does not retry it. Measured 2026-11-15: 100 from layer 10 and 160 from layer 16 generate on 5 of 5 seeds, 400 from layer 36 on 4 of 5, 500 from layer 48 on 5 of 5. In the sim 15 of 20 (length 100) and 18 of 20 (length 160) finish all three marbles; the job retries the rest. Tracks up to seven a layer are unchanged.
- Verified 2026-11-15: GPU Chromium on the 5311 preview (`e2e/tests/gen-worker.spec.mjs`, `target/perf/p6t2-gen.json`), at a 1-minute load of 13.6 before and 12.6 after: frames peaked at 16.8 ms idle (299 frames) and while generating (269), none dropped, against the 25 ms bar. Seeds 7, 72 and a length-80 track accepted in the worker in 795, 711 and 740 ms; seed 7 rejected once (a marble off the track at tick 2,221), its file's seed the accepted sub-seed. `make checkall-ci` passed on `ca6721f`.
- Supports dominate long tracks: one `blk_pillar` per layer under every elevated cell, so 100 pieces from layer 10 make 260 to 450 placements and 500 from layer 48 make 5,800 to 9,100 (before generator version 9's tall pillars, below). P6-T6's 500-piece track counts track pieces only, not supports (PRD P6).
- Validation job limits (2026-11-15 review, `docs/procgen-review-2026-11-15.md` phase 1): the tick limit counts track pieces only (2,400 ticks a marble plus 120 a track piece; supports had put a 500-piece track's limit at 1.6M ticks). A candidate is rejected the moment a marble leaves the track onto the open floor, and as stalled after two seconds with every marble released and none moving, instead of at the limit. Four exhausted searches in a row fail the job. Length 100 from layer 10, base 8: its first candidate is now rejected at tick 10,837 (limit 34,560) instead of running to 94,320. 500 from layer 48, base 1: accepted on its third candidate after an off-track and a stalled rejection, 47 s natively at a 1-minute load near 60.
- Generator version 8 (2026-11-15 review, phase 2). Clearance: `parble_core::validate_track` reports `OverheadBlocked` when a placement stands in the cell over an `L2` or `L3` channel (contract §5.1; the cell under a controlled drop's source is waived, which the all-pieces track's end stop under its third transfer needs), the search keeps that cell empty and a support column that would come down onto such a channel is left out, so the piece above spans that gap unsupported (Paul approved unsupported spans 2026-11-15). Speed model from `speed_census` (300 seeds, first marble, horizontal speed): straights keep their speed, block curves 0.87 to 0.90, the rope arch a quarter, the catch leaves at 0.17 m/s, the redirect is entered at 0.55 m/s or less, rail curves at 0.4 m/s or less (2 of 15 lost at 0.4 to 0.5 m/s) and the `L1` drop at 0.8 m/s or less (6 of 207 stalled above). Descent pacing: a step's descent is drawn so the steps average the levels left per piece left, and a finish is allowed from nine tenths of the length. Measured: first marble 295 of 300 at default (289 before); 3 marbles at length 100 from layer 10, 37 of 40 first candidates accepted by the job (34 of 40 before); delivered length 90% at every size (36 of 40, 90 of 100, 144 of 160, 450 of 500); 500 pieces from layers 42 and 48 on footprint 14 and 300 from layer 30 on footprint 8 generate on every seed tried. Golden `procgen_job.txt` regenerated, its default case now base 32 (seed 7's first candidate passes in version 8).
- Generator version 9 (2026-11-15 review, phase 5, built ahead of phases 3 and 4). Tall pillars: the kit gains `blk_pillar_h2`, `_h4` and `_h8` (contract §10; one piece 2, 4 or 8 layers tall, anchor in the top cell, structural, the same 80 collision triangles as `blk_pillar`), and `support.rs` stacks each column from its foot with the tallest that fits. The supports fill the cells the one-cell pillars did and the track pieces are unchanged (`tall_pillars_fill_the_cells_one_cell_pillars_would`). Measured 2026-11-15, native release, 1-minute load 130 to 390: 500 pieces from layer 48 on footprint 14, seeds 0 to 9, 1,201 to 1,566 placements (3,733 to 6,260 under version 8), and base 0's file is 175 KB; first marble 296 of 300 at default (295 under version 8: the same seeds 32, 256, 276 and 286 fail and 98 now finishes, its path differing in the last digit from 1.1 s on); 3 marbles at length 100 from layer 10, 37 of 40 (37 under version 8, seed 15 failing in place of 13). App wasm 14.14 MB gzip (14.12 before), generator wasm 1.34 MB. Golden `procgen_job.txt` regenerated: the same sub-seeds and rejections, new file hashes. Paul approved the look 2026-11-15 (`blender/kit/review/pillar-heights.png`). Open: the 500-piece track's frame timing at low load (`make web-gen500-perf`).

- Segments (2026-11-15 review, phase 6). `parble_procgen::segment`: `generate_segment(defs, seed, params, start, space, goal)` walks from any open port (`OpenPort`: cell, face, level, class, speed estimate, flat run, after-a-catch) through the cells in use (`Space`: occupied, overhead clearance, transfer fall cells, cells under a transfer's source) to a `Goal`, a finish on layer 0 or an open `L3` exit on a given layer. `start_segment` does the same from the hopper, and `generate` is now that one segment plus `assemble`, with the same output (generator version 8 unchanged, checked byte for byte on 7 presets). `segment_plan` splits a track into segments of about 64 pieces with the layers shared out, `generate_chain(defs, seed, params, seeds)` builds it from one seed a segment, and each segment's target is an even share of the pieces still needed (`chain_segment`), so a chain delivers 484 or more of 500 (mean 486 over 11 chains) where a whole track delivers 450. An open end needs two free cells ahead: 3 of 30 ends faced the plan's edge one cell away with a marble too fast for a curve. `GenError::GoalAbove` and `GenError::NoSegmentRoute` are the unreachable-goal errors.
- Segmented job. `parble_sim::procgen_job::SegmentedJob` generates the chain, runs the whole track from the hopper under the whole-track job's acceptance rule, and charges a failure to the first segment the failed marble had not rolled out of: that segment gets its next seed (`segment_seed`), the ones below are generated again, the ones above stay. A segment with no route from the end above it sends the segment above to its next seed. The accepted track has passed a whole run from scratch. A segment is not run alone from its entry because that is not the run the file gets (supports and chunk colliders differ, a marble arrives spinning), and the sim cannot be copied to run on from the entry. `parble-sim-wasm` exposes it as `generate_and_validate_segmented`, the worker takes `segmented: true` in its start message and `GenClient.generate` in its options.
- The file records the generator: `metadata.generator = { "version": 8, "segments": [seeds] }` (`segments` left out for a whole track, whose own `seed` regenerates it). The field is optional and the schema stays 1: files without it load and save byte for byte, and a build from before the field refuses a file that has it, as with `camera_zones`. A segmented track's `seed` is the seed the job started with.
- Measured 2026-11-15, native release, 1-minute load 135 to 175: 500 from layer 48 on footprint 14 accepted by the segmented job for bases 0 to 4 in 29.9, 65.0, 45.5, 43.4 and 26.5 s, on whole-track runs 1, 1, 1, 2 and 1 (one run rejected, marbles stalled on segment 2 by tick 41,436; two segments had no route from the end above them, which costs a generation and no run). The whole-track job on the same bases at load 132: 18.0, 25.2, 37.3, 33.5 and 22.8 s on 1, 1, 2, 2 and 1 candidates, for tracks of 450 pieces. Five bases do not separate the two rejection rates. The worker budget waits for mechanism sleeping (review phase 4). New golden line in `procgen_job.txt` (80 from layer 12, base 3, two segments), checked natively and by `determinism-wasm`; the two older lines keep their attempts and seeds and change their file hash, since the file now carries the generator stamp.
- Measured 2026-11-15 at low load on macdaddy (1-minute load 11.8 to 14.8). Tick rate, native release, 240 from layer 30 on footprint 12, one marble, seeds 0 to 3, 36,000 ticks at most: 108,964 ticks/s with no special pieces, 35,098 at 150 per mille (3.1x slower, 23 to 32 mechanisms) and 32,756 at 400 per mille. Sleeping mechanisms do not yet bring 150 per mille within 2x of the no-special rate (P6-T2e stays open). `make web-gen500-perf`, segmented 500 from layer 48 on footprint 14, base 0: accepted in the worker in 7.0 s on the first run (487 track pieces, 1,670 placements), frames while generating at most 16.8 ms and idle p95 16.8 ms at 2560x1440 High, but the place-and-undo edit costs a 50 ms frame (2 frames dropped) against a 25 ms bar, the same as the dry run at load 107 (P6-T2f stays open).
- Tick rate at 150 per mille, profiled 2026-11-15 (Instruments Time Profiler, finishing seeds 0 and 3 against no-special seeds 0, 2 and 3): 22.6 µs a tick against 10.0. Of the 12.6 µs extra, 3.9 went to the mechanism hash sample building `Instance::save`'s string-keyed map every 24 ticks (the rope arch's 96 rope keys formatted each time), 2.4 to the rest of `after_step`, 4.9 to the solver (the hinged parts' 48 extra iterations while awake) and 0.9 to collisions. The sample now reads `Mechanism::visit_state` into reused buffers and hashes the same bytes in the same key order (every golden unchanged), which takes the hash out of the profile. Giving a hinged part its extra iterations only while a marble is near was tried and dropped: the settling `mech_toggle_l3` then strands a mini marble on the curve after it in the all-pieces check. Seeds 1 and 2 at 150 per mille run all 36,000 ticks with the marble stalled against a hinged part (about one part awake every sample), which is the 48-iteration case itself; a validation run rejects such a stall after 480 ticks. The 2x bar is still not met: 39,385 against 109,780 ticks/s (2.8x) at load 42.

### P6-T4 — Fill-between
- In the editor the user picks two open ports. The generator routes a valid path between them (A* over port states with a height budget) and validates it with a headless run of that segment.
- Verify: unit tests on routing feasibility cases, including an unreachable pair (clear error). The Playwright flow passes.
- Depends on: P6-T1, P3-T4.
- Status (2026-11-15): the entry point is `parble_procgen::generate_segment` (start port, `Space`, `Goal`). Still to do: a `Goal` for a given target port with the router that steers to it (the walk descends at a pace and does not aim across the plan, so the A* over port states is its own search, sharing `Template`, `speed` and `Space`), a plan centred on the two ports instead of the origin, the editor's `Space` built from its track (`clearance_cells`, `controlled_drops`), and a headless run of the routed segment.

### P6-T5 — Infinite descent
- The worker generates and validates segments ahead of the followed marble. The main thread streams segment pieces and chunk colliders in.
- Segments far above the marble are recycled. The world is re-origined to keep float precision.
- Verify: a 30-minute soak in Playwright with bounded wasm memory (sample memory every minute; growth under 5 % after the first 5 minutes), no frame over budget from streaming, and determinism for a given seed.
- Depends on: P6-T2.
- Status (2026-11-15): segments chain through `generate_segment` with `Goal::OpenEnd`, each from the end of the one before. Still to do: validation that does not start at the hopper, since `SegmentedJob` reruns the whole track and an endless one has no finish. That needs the sim copied at a segment's entry (`Sim` is not `Clone`) or marbles started at the entry with the measured speed and spin, plus an arrival test at an open end in place of the finish cup. Also a `Space` that drops recycled segments, cell coordinates under re-origining, and supports for a track whose floor keeps moving down.
